# Impact and sustainability measurement contract

Status: domain pending. No measured benefit, market size, revenue or social-impact projection is currently defensible.

## Outcome specification

After verifying the problem, freeze the beneficiary, current task, pain point, unit of benefit and observation window. Select an outcome such as successful task completion or elapsed task time only when it fits the statement. Describe who could be excluded or harmed and collect that information alongside success.

For a lower-is-better time measure: improvement = baseline median time minus prototype median time; relative improvement = improvement / baseline median. Use the same task and comparable conditions, counterbalance task order, disclose participant count and report dispersion. A small convenience sample is formative evidence, not population-level impact. Registration count measures reach; it does not measure benefit.

For scenario planning only: annual gross time saved = eligible tasks per year × adoption fraction × valid-completion fraction × minutes saved per task. Net value subtracts onboarding, correction, support and operating costs. Leave every parameter null until sourced or explicitly assumed; show low/base/high sensitivity rather than one precise headline.

## Pilot design

Start with a small consenting participant group relevant to the real domain. Record baseline before training, task completion without hints, error frequency, time and experience barriers. Separate the team's own runs from independent users. Define success and stop conditions before collecting results. Do not collect personal or regulated information without the appropriate domain-specific handling design.

## Operating-cost ledger

Track fixed hosting/storage, variable provider cost per task, support hours, device/connectivity costs and ownership. Cite tariff date and currency. Compare to the incumbent process and sensitivity to load. Procurement, subscription, grant or institutional operation are alternatives to evaluate, not a chosen or validated business model.

## Environmental account

Prefer small assets, low idle compute, batching where appropriate and reuse of existing infrastructure. Measure transferred bytes and compute time before estimating energy. No claim of carbon savings without a stated energy model and emissions factor.
