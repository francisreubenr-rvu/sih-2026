# Evidence-first presentation strategy

Research date: 2026-09-08. Audience: SIH evaluators reviewing an RV University six-person undergraduate team. This is an authored delivery strategy, not an official SIH instruction.

## Grounding

SIH's historical official idea-selection dimensions include clarity, feasibility, impact and user experience. A successful pitch must expose the evidence for those dimensions, rather than simply name them. [Official historical guideline](https://sih.gov.in/letters/Guidelines-College-SPOC.pdf)

The Solar Masters sponsor-hosted winner account describes a methodology deck, video and technical documents alongside working demonstrations. This supports preparing complementary artifacts; it does not prove that a particular slide count or graphic style wins. [MathWorks account](https://blogs.mathworks.com/student-lounge/2025/06/13/innovation-meets-excellence-solar-masters-winning-journey-at-smart-india-hackathon-2024/?from=en)

A Reddit participant reports using the official template and diagrams to explain their system. Their separate speculation about ATS-based screening is unsupported and rejected. Treat the diagram advice as anecdotal, tested through rehearsal rather than accepted as a rule. [Participant discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/developersIndia/comments/1ev3y2r/)

Major League Hacking's own policy encourages demonstrating what was built. Its criteria and event rules differ from SIH, so only the demonstration principle transfers; its timing, prior-work and judging rules do not govern this entry. [MLH policy](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/standard-hackathon-rules.md)

## Artifact architecture

Respect the supplied PPTX's real section order and headings in the submission artifact. If its submission-slide limit conflicts with a requested 12–18-slide live delivery, create two explicitly named outputs: the template-format submission and the expanded presentation. Do not label an expanded deck as compliant with an incompatible slide limit.

Recommended live narrative: 15 slides, approximately 12 minutes excluding questions. This is a proposed pacing plan, not a measured rehearsal result.

| Slide | Judge question | Visual evidence | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is this and which problem does it solve? | Exact identity and current verification status | 25 s |
| 2 | Who loses time, access or money today? | Sourced burden with date and denominator | 40 s |
| 3 | Where does the current workflow fail? | Before-state journey and bottleneck | 40 s |
| 4 | What gap remains? | Sourced comparison on material dimensions | 45 s |
| 5 | What is the proposed change? | One promise and testable mechanism | 40 s |
| 6 | How does it work? | Input to action architecture and trust boundaries | 55 s |
| 7 | Can I use it? | Real complete workflow demonstration | 90 s |
| 8 | What happens when it fails? | Controlled error and recovery | 45 s |
| 9 | What is proved? | Actual results, environment and limits | 50 s |
| 10 | Can it operate reliably? | Run, protect and bound the system | 45 s |
| 11 | Can this team deliver? | Proposed roles, artifacts and milestone strip | 45 s |
| 12 | Who benefits? | Baseline and pilot outcome model | 45 s |
| 13 | Can the pilot be sustained? | Adoption, cost and decision threshold | 40 s |
| 14 | Where is the evidence? | Primary references and inference labels | 50 s |
| 15 | What is the next decision? | Bounded next step and honest open dependencies | 45 s |

Total planned narration: 700 seconds (11 minutes 40 seconds). Keep a 20-second transition allowance for a 12-minute presentation. A 10-minute slot needs a shortened script; a 15-minute slot leaves discussion room, not permission to add text-heavy slides.

## Make each claim inspectable

Every quantitative slide needs a claim label: **sourced external fact**, **measured prototype result**, or **scenario projection**. A projected social-impact percentage must show the assumption behind it. A placeholder measurement should say “not measured,” never a plausible-looking number.

Every architecture component must be classed as implemented, simulated or planned in speaker notes. Use screenshots only from the delivered application. Show a source below external statistics and keep an expanded reference register in the technical appendix or companion document. Avoid decorative institution, government or customer logos that imply endorsement.

Use one dominant visual per slide and an assertion title that communicates the conclusion. Prefer a short sequence of visible states over an animation that hides system behavior. Keep large readable type, adequate contrast, generous spacing and labels on all diagrams. All information needed to understand the solution must survive a static PDF export.

## Demonstration choreography

Prepare a reproducible seed dataset and record the exact application version. Start at a stable state, complete a real action, show its persisted result, then trigger one safe error and recover. Keep narration synchronized to what judges can see. Avoid changing code, accounts or network configuration during the main demonstration.

Fallback order: live local/server demonstration → local screen recording of that same build → screenshot storyboard with explicit disclosure. A recording is evidence of the recorded workflow, not proof of uptime or live integration. Test playback without internet and make the recording's duration and file location part of the rehearsal checklist.

## Team delivery and evidence

Use one primary narrator and one demo operator to reduce handoff cost; each subsystem owner answers technical questions. Assignments are proposed work allocation, not evidence of existing expertise. Team biographies must use only user-provided facts. Portrait placeholders remain honest initials until licensed, consented team photographs are supplied.

Team capability evidence should be a commit, test report, architecture decision, research synthesis or rehearsal contribution. Do not invent internships, awards, customer interviews, prototypes or role experience for Gopreet, Hiranmayi, Varun, Koushaik, Francis or Niharika.

## Rehearsal measurements

These are internal metrics, not official SIH scores:

| Metric | Method | Suggested acceptance target |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative coherence | Independent reviewer rates problem → gap → mechanism → proof → adoption links, each 0–2; total / 10 | At least 0.8, with no zero link |
| Judge comprehension proxy | After one viewing, reviewer answers 5 factual questions about user, pain, mechanism, proof and next step | At least 4/5 correct |
| Evidence coverage | Supported material claims / all material claims | 100%; separate assumptions |
| Demonstration completion | Successful seeded end-to-end attempts / total attempts | 3/3 before recording |
| Time control | Recorded spoken presentation duration | 10–15 minutes, or actual organizer slot |
| Persuasion probability | Requires calibrated historic outcomes and a representative evaluator sample | **Not estimable from current evidence** |

Do not relabel a checklist score as a probability of winning. An anonymous internal reviewer response may measure comprehension or willingness to support a pilot; it does not estimate national jury selection probability.

## Closing proposition

Close on a bounded next step: evaluate the demonstrated workflow against the stated pilot measures. The product's strongest promise is the improvement it can prove. The team should be able to explain both what works and the next condition that must be satisfied before real-world adoption.
