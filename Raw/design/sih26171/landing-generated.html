build Active Engineering Constraints & Known Limits

Scientific precision requires absolute honesty about boundaries. Sightline does not claim universal coverage. The following vectors are active testbed priorities:

1. Adversarial CSS De-synchronization: Elements hidden via zero-opacity pointer pseudo-layers can cause coordinate misalignment during the ACT stage.
2. Canvas-Rendered Custom Fonts: Custom WebGL/Canvas UI controls without accessible DOM trees require secondary OCR passes, degrading latency by ~85ms.
3. Closed Shadow Roots: Browser runtime extensions cannot inspect closed shadow trees without explicit browser instrumentation flags enabled.
Target resolution scheduled for 11 September Milestone testbed lock.
