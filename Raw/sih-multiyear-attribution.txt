# Attribution

An archival collection of publicly available Smart India Hackathon problem
statements. The problem statement text is the work of its original authors —
the Government of India ministries, departments, state governments and
organisations that published it, and the Smart India Hackathon / AICTE. No
authorship or ownership of that text is claimed here.

Text is reproduced as published. Only Markdown structure, whitespace, stray
markup and character-encoding errors were normalised; no wording was changed,
summarised or rewritten. No logos, images, graphics or branding are included.
